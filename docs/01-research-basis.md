# Research Basis

This document records literature, standards, datasets, equations, empirical findings, and other evidence used to justify the quantitative simulation model.

No quantitative parameter should be treated as literature-supported unless its source and interpretation are recorded here or in the parameter-evidence directory.

## Research Areas

- Pedestrian free-flow speed
- Pedestrian speed distributions
- Density-speed relationships
- Corridor and bottleneck flow
- Personal-space and collision behavior
- Route choice
- Congestion representation
- Evacuation behavior
- Dynamic rerouting
- Hazard representation
- Simulation timestep and numerical behavior
- Validation approaches
